Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes — every device on the floor is powered and playable. Bring your own music, pair your phone, and take your time. The listening room is yours for as long as you need it.

Do you repair devices bought elsewhere?

We do. Our bench handles all major brands, whether or not they came from us. Diagnosis is free, and we'll always quote before any work begins.

What's your returns policy?

Fourteen days, unopened or as-new, with proof of purchase. Faulty items are covered by the manufacturer warranty plus our own two-year support and in-house repair.

How does trade-in work?

Bring the device along with its charger. We test it in store and quote on the spot — the credit goes straight against your next purchase. No obligation to buy.

How do I find you?

We're at 30 Giltress Street in Rollington Town, Kingston 2. Call 876-633-9251 if you need directions — we're happy to talk you in.
