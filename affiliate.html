Affiliate Program

Sell with us, from anywhere.

Bring the products and services you already stand behind. We set you up with a referral code, a partner listing and customers who trust the shop they walk into.

Applications are read at the shop, not by a script. Most people hear back within two working days.

How it works

Four steps, no fine print.

Short enough to explain across the counter — and the same for everyone who joins.

01

Apply

Tell us what you sell, where you sell it and who you sell it to. A few lines is enough to start the conversation.

02

Meet

We sit down at the counter, or on a call, and talk through your products, your services and the customers you already look after.

03

Set up

You get a referral code and link, a listing on this page, and artwork if you want to advertise in store.

04

Earn

We track the referrals you send, settle on the schedule we agreed, and tell you what's moving so you can push what sells.

What you can promote

Your work, in front of our customers.

Bring one thing or all four. The arrangement is agreed with you, in writing, before anything goes live.

Products

Stock and accessories

Devices and accessories you already carry. We refer the customer, handle the sale at the counter, and share it on the terms we agreed.

Services

Repairs, setup, installation

The work you do best — repairs, networking, mounting, training. When a customer asks for it, we pass the job to you.

Brand

A partner listing

Your name, what you do and how to reach you, listed here and at the counter so people can put a face to the work.

Advertising

In-store and on screen

Shelf space, a QR code and screen time in the demo room, booked in blocks so your product is seen by people already shopping.

Advertising

What partner ads look like.

Flyers we print, post and share on WhatsApp — three from the current stack. Partners can have their own made to match, and we'll put them in the rotation.

Who it suits

Independent repairers, installers and resellers across Kingston, plus makers and creators with an audience that trusts them. You don't need a shopfront — you need work you'd put your name on.

What we ask

A real business or a real body of work, customers answered the same day, and prices that stay honest after you join. We keep the directory small on purpose.
